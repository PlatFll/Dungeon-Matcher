# Review Stop Points

Astra should stop and report for review at these checkpoints:

## Stop 1
After structural base tiles are built and tested in a plain art-only scene.

## Stop 2
After gate/arch hero set is generated, cleaned, and assembled.

## Stop 3
After premium UI hero pieces are generated and cleaned.

## Stop 4
After props are generated and placed.

## Stop 5
After the full assembled art-only dungeon zone scene is complete and validation passes.

At each stop provide:
- preview screenshots
- asset list completed
- what used PixelLab vs manual cleanup
- known issues if any
- confirmation that nothing has been merged
