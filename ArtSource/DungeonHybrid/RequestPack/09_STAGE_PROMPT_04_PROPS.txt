STAGE 04 — PROPS / DRESSING

Use PixelLab for each prop family separately.

Generate these one by one:
- torch
- chain
- banner
- barrel
- vase / jar
- skull pile / bones
- small rubble / debris
- optional padlock / hanging ornament

Prompt requirements for each:
- native pixel art prop
- clean silhouette
- premium but readable detail
- matches fantasy dungeon zone
- no blur
- no over-smoothed shading
- no giant scene composition
- transparent or easily isolatable background if supported

After generation:
- clean in LibreSprite
- normalize palette
- trim and align
- export consistent asset naming
- place in Unity scene for dressing validation
