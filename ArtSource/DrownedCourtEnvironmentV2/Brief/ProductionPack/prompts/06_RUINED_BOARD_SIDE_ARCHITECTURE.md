## Generate families
- Tall matching submerged stone columns along left/right board margins (repeatable shaft/cap/base), optional ribbed fluting/rune relief.
- Reef-support stone ledges/crevices integrated into column geometry; tiny shell patches growing in seams.
- A few long green kelp fronds with actual attached holdfasts inside stone cracks; isolate frond tips for later ambient motion.
- Pink coral branching from stone shelf sockets. Selective not entire height.
- A few bubble vent outlets integrated at cracks, not free-floating rings on frames.

### PixelLab description A — side stone pillar
> Connected tall underwater ruin pillar for side gutter of pixel-art match-3 board, structural teal stone masonry from cap to base with built-in stone ledges, carved slits and mortar joints; side-on 2D view, clean dark outlines, large stone blocks, deep-water shadows. Limited barnacle and seaweed base attachment points; no full-scene composition, no floating stickers, no bubbles painted over the board.

### PixelLab description B — kelp attached to column
> Two or three flowing kelp blades growing from one rock crevice and held by a small dark-green root/holdfast on ancient sea stone, dark olive-teal body and selectively lit upper tips, 2D underwater pixel art, strong continuous silhouette, designed as overlay anchored to the side of a pillar. Avoid leaf-wall foliage, no background or UI.

### PixelLab description C — coral grown in cracks
> Compact branching coral colony in muted pink/magenta emerging from a visible notch on weathered stone, multiple stems sharing a clear base, a few bright tips, heavy at bottom and tapering above, crisp pixels and restrained shading. Environmental growth asset on ruin masonry, not random colored blobs.

## Structural rule
Corals/kelp must read as actually **growing from** the stone supports. Avoid two separate vertical stone pieces or 'sticker' decorations detached by 1px. Maintain quiet dark board cell interior.

## Validation
Proof collage at native 1x with entire pillar side, overlaid holdfast attachment points, and all silhouettes separate from board gem boundaries. Stop.
