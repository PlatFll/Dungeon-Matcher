## Important Forest lesson
Build **one cohesive main rock formation at bottom left and one at bottom right**, connected into the upper side masonry/sea floor. Do NOT create two disjoint stacked stones per side or paste mushroom-like coral sprigs randomly on flat stones. Coral, seaweed and shells need believable attachment crevices and surface shadows.

## Build separately
- Left corner unified submerged boulder/ruin base supporting low coral/barnacles/shells and optional starfish.
- Right corner unified boulder/ruin mass with a partly buried ancient skull/helmet or relic, selective seaweed and coral.
- Supporting pebbles/rubble transition on the base line and dark quiet gaps behind the functional central HUD.
