### PixelLab description A — coral pocket
> Game-ready pixel-art underwater reef pocket rooted on carved sea-temple stone: one magenta branching coral, one small mauve sponge and several pale clustered barnacles attached to a single believable stone crevice. Isolated transparent prop, readable chunky shading, no fluorescent aquarium excess, no unconnected floating fragments, no HUD/characters.

### PixelLab description B — barnacles and shell group
> Small underwater barnacle grouping, three to seven off-white rounded shell collars fused to a common patch of teal rock, weathered and shadowed; suitable for ledge placement, not free white dots. Crisp simple fantasy pixel-art prop at current native pixel density, transparent background.

### PixelLab description C — starfish
> One muted coral-red sea star attached flat to the top/front of an ancient teal stone ledge, five chunky arms, sparse upper-left highlights and dark material separation, isolated map prop, no excessive microdots, 2D pixel art.

## Validation
Actively remove weak or redundant assets. Compose asymmetrically with negative space, not 2 identical mirrored coral columns. No generic underwater aquarium or overdecoration. Stop.
