## Production assets
- Calm horizontal ancient stone walkway for enemy feet with modest irregular slab joints and occasional dark stone seams.
- Repeatable stone top modules and side-view **front-facing fascia blocks**, plus left/right termination and corner variants.
- Small intentional rubble margin, not pebble carpet. Underwater material colors shall match column stone.

### PixelLab description A — stone floor tiles
> Side-view pixel-art underwater palace battle platform tile family: long simple connected blue-teal stone slabs with broad smooth faces, shallow dark grout seams, scarce worn chips and sand deposits, cool top-left highlights. Horizontal walkway for three enemy silhouettes. Create compatible repeatable floor tiles suitable for a 2D platformer, NO isometric view, NO receding perspective grid, no dense tiny cracks, no coral covering combat feet, no characters or UI. Game-native source pixels.

### PixelLab description B — stone platform front
> Modular front vertical side of a submerged royal stone platform, chunky turquoise-teal rectangular cut blocks and deep recessed grout, subtle worn chips, top lip catchlight and darker undersides. Straight frontal 2D side view with seamless left-right repeated segments and matching corners. No full wall, no perspective floor.
