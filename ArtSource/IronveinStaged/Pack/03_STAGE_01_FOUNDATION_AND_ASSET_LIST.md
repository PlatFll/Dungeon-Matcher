# Stage 01 — Foundation and Asset List

## Goal
Break the scene into asset families before generation begins.

## Required output
Create a production list covering:
1. top HUD cave background kit
2. portrait frame kit
3. board frame kit
4. side pillar kit
5. mine lighting kit
6. mining prop kit
7. lower-environment support kit
8. ambient detail kit
9. assembly plan

## Instructions
- Inspect the composition target.
- Write the asset list in concrete terms.
- Identify which parts can be modular.
- Identify which parts must be unique hero props.
- Do not generate production art yet until the list is locked.

## Approval standard
The list should make the whole scene feel buildable in focused chunks.
