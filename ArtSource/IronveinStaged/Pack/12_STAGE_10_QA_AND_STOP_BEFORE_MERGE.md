# Stage 10 — QA and Stop Before Merge

## Validation checklist
- Does the zone clearly read as dwarven excavation / mine?
- Is it stronger than the previous weak attempt?
- Do the frame systems still match the same game family?
- Is the board readable?
- Is the portrait frame readable?
- Are the props supportive rather than noisy?
- Are there no blurry AI textures?
- Are there no disconnected elements?
- Do lighting and palette feel controlled?
- Does the scene feel complete?

## Stop condition
When all stages are complete:
- stop before merge
- present review screenshots
- present asset summary
- present what was generated / cleaned / integrated
- state clearly that nothing has been merged
