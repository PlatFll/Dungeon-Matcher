# Stage 08 — Assembly and Scene Composition

## Goal
Assemble the approved asset families into a cohesive Ironvein Excavation scene in Unity.

## Assembly order
1. top HUD environment background
2. portrait frame
3. board frame
4. side pillars/supports
5. mining props
6. bottom environment dressing
7. polish pass

## Composition rules
- The scene must feel richer and stronger than the weak art-only attempt.
- It should read clearly as one of the same game's zones.
- Structural weight should be visible.
- Lantern glow should be warm but controlled.
- The board should remain readable.
- The top scene should feel like a real excavation chamber, not a flat wallpaper.
- The scene should not feel empty.
- The scene should not feel overstuffed.

## Rule
If assembly reveals a missing asset family, generate that family before final polish.
Do not accept a weak, underdeveloped result.
