# PixelLab Usage Notes

## General best practice
- Generate one family at a time.
- Prefer modular, reusable outputs.
- Avoid giant prompts that mix too many unrelated asset needs.
- When a family comes out weak, regenerate only that family.
- Keep notes on best-performing prompt phrasing.

## Recommended generation rhythm
1. prompt
2. generate
3. reject weak outputs
4. keep strongest output
5. clean/recolor
6. integrate
7. validate
8. move on

## Why this workflow
The weak result came from trying to solve too much at once. This pack deliberately slows the process down so PixelLab can spend attention on each asset family.
