# Global Art Direction — Ironvein Excavation

## Style targets
- Same game family as the approved Dungeon and Magical Forest zones
- Crisp pixel art
- Clean cluster control
- No blur
- No muddy over-detailing
- No smeared AI textures
- Readable forms first
- Decorative detail second

## Ironvein-specific identity
- Dwarven mining cavern
- Excavation tunnels
- Stone-cut construction
- Iron braces and rivets
- Lanterns / warm fire glow
- Timber supports
- Ore seams and crystals
- Rail tracks / mine cart language
- Sturdy masonry and carved architectural rhythm

## Value / color behavior
- Deep cool cave shadows as the base
- Warm orange-yellow glow accents
- Purple-pink UI health bars and resonance can remain consistent with the game
- Metal should read cooler than timber
- Timber should read sturdy and workmanlike, not decorative fantasy wood

## Shape language
- geometric
- solid
- reinforced
- cut stone, not organic curves
- timber beams should feel structural
- metal corners / rivets should feel functional

## Do not do
- no soft painterly cave walls
- no overgrown nature shapes
- no random clutter spam
- no excessive tiny noise pixels
- no one-shot full scene generation as final production
