STAGE 06 — PILLARS, BUTTRESSES, ARCH SUPPORTS, EDGE MASONRY
=========================================================
Read shared rules and latest checkpoint. ONLY structural vertical architecture, not props/UI.

Style brief: chunky side-view vertical support columns or pilasters under/around platform and gate, blocky cool purple stone, modest top-left shading and recessed mortar; sparse small architectural capitals and foot blocks. These should make wall/platform geometry feel built, not just wallpaper. Do not create giant red-orange decorative pillars, nor change the stage layout.

1. Generate at least one native architectural style source through PixelLab (unless an earlier accepted PixelLab generation already produced the whole needed support family; prove lineage).
2. Build repeatable pillar core, capital/foot, corner, wall/ledge edge/cap variants in LibreSprite as few meaningful pieces, not dozens of boilerplate empty assets.
3. Use the existing 2D arch/platform scene to set correct overlaps and contacts; no isolated chunks beside the board.
4. Validate repeat continuity, geometry, and subdued palette. Side gutters should eventually run uninterrupted through board/bottom HUD.

ACCEPT: strong coherent structural dungeon architecture, no noisy bricks or seam breakage. STOP. NEXT: 07.
