# Review Stop Points

Astra must stop at these points and show work before moving on.

1. After Stage 1 asset map
2. After Stage 2 top battle background
3. After Stage 3 portrait frame
4. After Stage 4 battle floor / separator
5. After Stage 5 board frame
6. After Stage 6 board-side dressing
7. After Stage 7 bottom HUD environment
8. After Stage 8 atmosphere pass
9. After Stage 9 final assembly

## Stop behavior
At every stop point:
- summarize what was generated
- show the current outputs
- note any weak areas that may need another generation pass
- wait for review

## Merge rule
