# Stage 3 — Snow Portrait Frame Kit

## Objective
Generate the player portrait panel so it belongs to the snow zone while staying in the same UI family.

## Desired visual result
A portrait panel with:
- dark cold blue interior masonry / brick backing
- sturdy timber verticals and crosspieces
- metal corner plates / braces and bolts
- snow caps resting naturally on top edges
- small icicles and frost buildup where appropriate
- banner space near the upper area
- enough structure to feel premium and game-ready

## PixelLab prompt direction
Describe a vertical portrait frame kit for a pixel-art fantasy match-3 battle screen:
- cold timber beams
- steel plates and bolts
- blue-gray brick interior panel
- fresh snow resting on upper beam and ledges
- subtle icicles
- clean rectangular structure
- crisp pixel rendering
- readable and not over-cluttered

Generate multiple versions if needed. Keep the frame readable and compatible with Rattlebones.

## Output expected at end of stage
- portrait frame kit or full portrait panel plate
- optional modular corner / beam pieces if useful

## Stop condition
Stop and present the portrait frame results.
