# Art Direction Summary

## Zone fantasy
A frozen mountain pass / fortress approach high in the snow.

## Emotional read
- crisp
- cold
- adventurous
- exposed to winter air
- elevated, scenic, and slightly heroic

## Material language
- icy blue-gray stone
- dark desaturated blue brick / masonry for panel interiors
- weathered timber posts and beams
- iron / steel braces and bolts
- bright white snow caps and drifts
- icicles and frozen buildup
- rope posts / fence structures
- hanging lanterns with warm orange light
- occasional blue ice crystals
- snowy conifers and distant alpine architecture in the background

## Composition target
### Top battle area
- left portrait frame built into the environment
- central / right battle background showing mountain pass scenery
- snowy ledge / battle floor beneath the enemies
- distant bridge / path and snow-covered structures behind
- enough depth that the scene feels outdoors, not like a flat wall

### Board area
- strong wood-and-iron board frame
- snow resting naturally on top edges and ledges
- side dressing that feels like a continuation of the frozen cliffside, not random pasted props
- hanging lanterns / banners / posts integrated with the board surround

### Lower zone / shared HUD area
- preserve functional layout
- enrich it with snowbanks, crystals, posts, rope, crates, and carved stone / timber supports
- avoid clutter that fights the main HUD

## Style rules
- crisp pixel art
- no blurry painterly rendering
- controlled detail
- readable silhouettes
- coherent palette
- consistency with the game's established gem brightness and character readability

## Animation opportunities
Prepare separate elements where helpful for later subtle motion:
- lantern sway
- falling snow particles
- slight flag/banner flutter
- icicle drip if desired
