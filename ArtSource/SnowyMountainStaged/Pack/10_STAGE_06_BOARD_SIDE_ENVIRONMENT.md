# Stage 6 — Board-Side Environmental Dressing

## Objective
Generate the snow-zone side dressing around the board so the zone feels complete and in-family with the forest and underwater zones.

## Desired visual result
Around the left and right sides of the board, create integrated environmental dressing such as:
- timber posts with hanging lanterns
- red banners with white stag / mountain-emblem motif
- snowbanks and built-up drifts
- rock outcrops
- blue ice crystal clusters
- rope posts or fence continuations if useful
- small structural supports so the scene feels architected, not pasted together

## Important rule
These side elements must feel integrated with the board frame and the scene. They must not look randomly pasted beside the board.

- small banners or cloth accents
- alpine fortress materials
- strong vertical composition flanking the board
- crisp pixel art and clean silhouette separation

## Output expected at end of stage

- right side dressing asset(s)
