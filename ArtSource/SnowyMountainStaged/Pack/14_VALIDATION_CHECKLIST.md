# Validation Checklist

Use this at the end of every stage.

## Global quality
- [ ] Crisp native pixel art
- [ ] No blurry scaling
- [ ] No noisy over-detail
- [ ] Palette feels coherent with the rest of the game
- [ ] Zone identity is obvious

## Functional readability
- [ ] Enemies remain readable over the background
- [ ] Gems remain easy to distinguish
- [ ] Board border does not distract from play
- [ ] Shared HUD remains usable
- [ ] Portrait area remains clear

## Snow zone specifics
- [ ] Snow reads as natural buildup, not white paint
- [ ] Wood / iron / stone relationship feels believable
- [ ] Icicles and frost are used tastefully
- [ ] Lantern warmth contrasts nicely with the cold palette
- [ ] Board-side dressing feels integrated, not pasted on
- [ ] Lower environment feels finished but not cluttered
