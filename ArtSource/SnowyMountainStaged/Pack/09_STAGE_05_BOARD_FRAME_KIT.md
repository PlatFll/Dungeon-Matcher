# Stage 5 — Board Frame Kit

## Objective
Generate the main board frame so it feels like a premium snow-zone board surround.

## Desired visual result
- heavy wood frame strengthened with iron plates and bolts
- snow sitting naturally along upper edges and exposed ledges
- controlled icicles and frost accents
- corners that feel intentional and structural
- enough zone flavor to look special, but still clean enough for gameplay

## Suggested structure
- timber side posts
- timber top and bottom rails
- iron plate corners / brackets
- small structural details consistent with the top portrait frame kit

## PixelLab prompt direction
Describe a square / near-square board frame for a vertical fantasy match-3 game:
- cold wood and iron construction
- crisp pixel art
- snow caps on horizontal members
- icy weathering
- premium handcrafted frame
- readable dark inner border around the gem board
- no excessive clutter inside the board play area

## Output expected at end of stage
- board frame kit or large frame plate
- placed test in scene

## Stop condition
Stop and present the board frame before side-dressing is added.
