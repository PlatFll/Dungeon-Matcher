## Objective
Generate the snow mountain top battle background so it feels like an outdoor frozen pass, not an old flat UI scene.

## Desired visual result
A scenic frozen mountain route with:
- towering snowy cliffs framing the scene
- layered distant mountains
- a raised bridge / rope-fenced path receding into the distance
- snow-covered pines
- hints of a fortress or stone structure in the distance
- cool blue lighting overall
- clean depth separation from foreground battle floor

- distant bridge/path with rope posts
- tall snowy pine trees
- subtle ruin / tower silhouettes in the background
- readable negative space behind enemies
- crisp pixel-art lighting, controlled detail, no blur
