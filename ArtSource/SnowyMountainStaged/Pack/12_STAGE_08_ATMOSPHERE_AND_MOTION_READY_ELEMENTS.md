# Stage 8 — Atmosphere and Motion-Ready Elements

## Objective
Generate or prepare the small atmospheric details that make the zone feel alive.

## Candidate details
- falling snow particle overlays
- tiny drifting snow motes
- banner flutter-ready frames if applicable
- lantern sway-ready separated assets
- small icicle drip or sparkle accents

## Important rule
These should enhance the scene, not overwhelm it.

## PixelLab prompt direction
Describe small atmospheric pixel-art effects for a frozen mountain battle screen:
- subtle snowfall
- tiny drifting white flecks
- faint cold glints
- optional soft lantern glow elements
- minimal, tasteful animation-friendly pieces

## Output expected at end of stage
- particle/effect assets or separated motion-ready layers
- in-scene atmosphere test

## Stop condition
Stop and present the atmosphere pass.
