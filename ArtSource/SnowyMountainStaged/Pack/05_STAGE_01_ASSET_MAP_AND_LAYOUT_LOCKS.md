# Stage 1 — Asset Map and Layout Locks

## Objective
Before generating art, establish the exact list of assets, reusable strips, and assembly zones required for the snow mountain scene.

## What to do
- Review the target scene and the family-style references.
- Lock the layout areas:
  - top HUD area
  - portrait panel area
  - top battle background area
  - enemy floor / battle ledge
  - separator region between battle area and board
  - board frame
  - board-side dressing
  - lower shared HUD area
  - bottom-left and bottom-right environmental filler
- Create the asset checklist and indicate which elements should be generated as:
  - full plates
  - modular pieces
  - reusable side kits
  - atmospheric overlays

## Output expected at end of stage
- written asset map
- list of all planned generated assets
- notes on what will be modular vs one-piece
- no merge

## Stop condition
After the asset map is done, stop and present the plan before generating Stage 2 art.
