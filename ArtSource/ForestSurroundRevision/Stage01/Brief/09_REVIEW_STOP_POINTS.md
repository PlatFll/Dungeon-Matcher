# Review Stop Points

Stop and present evidence at the following points:

## Stop 1
After Stage 1 board-side assets are assembled in preview form.

## Stop 2
After Stage 2 lower-side assets are assembled in preview form.

## Stop 3
After Stage 3 accent assets are created and tested in scene.

## Stop 4
After final Stage 4 assembly and polish.

## At each stop provide
- preview images
- short summary of what changed
- note of what was preserved
- note of what still feels weak, if anything

No merge until final review approval.
