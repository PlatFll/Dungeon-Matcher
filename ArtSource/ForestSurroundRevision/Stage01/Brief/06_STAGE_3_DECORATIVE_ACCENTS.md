# Stage 3 — Decorative Accents and Magical Life

## Objective
Add the finishing accents that make the forest feel enchanted, without making it messy.

## Wanted accents
- blue wisps / spirit lights
- tiny floating glow motes
- small mushroom groups
- occasional tiny flowers
- sparse drifting leaf accents
- vine tips that could sway later

## Animation-minded note
Design some accents so they can later support subtle motion:
- wisps blinking in and out
- leaf tips swaying
- vine tips moving gently

Still art quality comes first.

## Usage rule
Use accents sparingly. The scene should not become noisy.

## PixelLab guidance
Generate accents as separate small assets where practical, so they can be placed intentionally rather than baked into everything.

## Exact prompt language suggestion
Create small decorative pixel-art assets for an enchanted forest UI: glowing blue wisps, tiny magical motes, mushroom clusters, flower tufts, mossy details, and small vine or leaf accent pieces. Match a crisp fantasy pixel-art style. Keep them clean, readable, and subtle.
