# Art Direction Delta — What Needs to Change

The current Astra forest version is already strong in the top HUD. The weak area is the environmental dressing around the board and around the lower HUD sides.

## Desired correction
Shift the side environment from:
- dense leafy masses
- bushy plant walls
- too much foliage volume

toward:
- thick twisting roots
- woody trunk structures
- mossy bark surfaces
- stone anchors near the bottom
- selective leaf clusters instead of full leafy walls
- mushrooms and flowers as accent clusters
- subtle glowing wisps / spirit lights

## Target feeling
A magical forest that feels:
- enchanted
- alive
- old
- rooted
- gently animated by wind/magic
- structured and readable

## Important hierarchy
Primary forms:
1. thick roots and woody trunk shapes
2. stone bases / embedded rocks

Secondary forms:
3. moss patches
4. selective leaf clusters
5. hanging vines

Accent forms:
6. mushroom clusters
7. small flowers
8. floating wisps / glowing particles

## Avoid
- hedge-like walls
- fluffy overgrown leaf masses
- random clutter
- too many small disconnected details
- losing the clean silhouette around the board
