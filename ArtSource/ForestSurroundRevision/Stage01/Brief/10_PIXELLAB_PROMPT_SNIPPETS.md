# PixelLab Prompt Snippets

Use these as building blocks, not necessarily as one giant prompt.

---

## A. Board-side roots
Create pixel-art enchanted forest side-border assets for a match-3 battle board. Use thick twisting roots, woody trunks, mossy bark, and occasional embedded stones. Keep foliage secondary: only selective leaf clusters and a few flowers. Avoid bushy hedge-like foliage or dense leafy walls. Clean silhouettes, crisp fantasy pixel-art, readable large shapes.

---

## B. Lower side clusters
Create pixel-art lower side environmental clusters for an enchanted forest UI. Use thick root bases, mossy stones, mushroom clusters, small flowers, and a few vine tips. The result should feel like living forest growth grounding the lower HUD. Avoid clutter and dense foliage walls.

---

## C. Wisps and magical accents
Create small pixel-art magical forest accent assets: glowing blue wisps, spirit motes, tiny floating lights, mushroom groups, flower tufts, and small leaf/vine accents. Keep them subtle and readable.

---

## D. Assembly intent note
Keep the current top HUD and upper battle section unchanged. Only revise the board surround and the lower side surroundings so they move closer to the target mockup.
