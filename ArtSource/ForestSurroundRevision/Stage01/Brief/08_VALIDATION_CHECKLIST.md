# Validation Checklist

Use this checklist before marking a stage ready.

## Top HUD lock
- [ ] Top HUD preserved
- [ ] Upper battle area preserved
- [ ] No accidental redesign in the locked region

## Board-side revision quality
- [ ] Side art is root/trunk dominant
- [ ] Leaf masses reduced
- [ ] No bush-wall appearance
- [ ] Board remains readable
- [ ] Side silhouettes are clean

## Lower HUD-side revision quality
- [ ] Lower sides feel fuller / less empty
- [ ] Thick roots present
- [ ] Stone anchors present where appropriate
- [ ] Mushrooms / flowers used tastefully
- [ ] Lower section feels grounded and alive

## Magical forest feel
- [ ] Wisps / magical motes support the enchanted mood
- [ ] Environment feels mystical, not generic
- [ ] Decorative life is present but not cluttered

## Pixel quality
- [ ] Crisp pixel edges
- [ ] No blurry scaling
- [ ] No broken seams
- [ ] No torn pixels
- [ ] No misaligned tiles
- [ ] Shading remains readable and controlled

## Match to target direction
- [ ] Closer to target mockup than the current version in the revised areas
- [ ] Top remains as strong as current Astra version
