# Stage 2 — Revise Lower HUD Side Surroundings

## Objective
Make the lower left and lower right side areas near the bottom HUD feel fuller, more alive, and closer to the mockup.

## Problem in current version
The lower section can feel empty or too flat if it does not have enough rooted environmental support.

## Desired additions
Add side environmental support using:
- thicker root bases
- stone anchors / rock clusters
- mushrooms near the base
- flower tufts
- hanging vine ends
- subtle moss growth

## Design principle
The lower sides should feel like the forest is **growing around and holding the lower HUD area**, not swallowing it.

## PixelLab guidance
Generate lower side clusters with a clear big-to-small structure:
1. large roots / trunk masses
2. rocks / stone anchors
3. mushrooms
4. flowers / leaves
5. subtle magical accents

## Exact prompt language suggestion
Create pixel-art lower side environmental clusters for an enchanted forest match-3 UI. Use thick roots, stone anchors, moss, mushroom clusters, small flowers, and a few hanging vines. The sides should feel grounded, magical, and alive, but still clean and readable. Avoid dense bush masses. Use strong root silhouettes and clustered base dressing.
