# QA and Approval Rules

Before marking any stage complete, check:

1. **Crispness**
   - no blurry upscale look
   - no smeared edges
   - no muddy pixel clusters

2. **Material clarity**
   - wood, metal, crystal, and rock should read distinctly

3. **Integration**
   - drills must look built into the frame
   - crystal accents must feel mounted, not pasted
   - lower support art must belong to the scene

4. **Symmetry / structure**
   - left and right sides should feel intentionally constructed
   - spacing should be clean and readable

5. **Style consistency**
   - should fit the same family as the Dungeon and Forest zones
   - should feel like the same game

6. **Readability**
   - gems and gameplay grid remain the priority
   - lower-half decoration cannot distract too much
