# Art Direction

## Core lower-half identity
The lower half should feel like a **reinforced mine machine** built into the game board.

Think:
- wooden beams as structural rails
- dark forged iron plates and brackets
- riveted construction
- amber crystal insets for zone flavor
- drill modules mounted into the board-side assemblies
- mine machinery energy, but still readable and elegant

## Board frame target
The board frame should feel like a specialized mining apparatus rather than a generic frame.

### Desired properties
- chunky readable silhouette
- wood + metal combination
- metal corner plates and braces
- amber crystal diamonds / lamps embedded as accent nodes
- side-mounted drill modules pointing inward toward the board
- drills spaced apart cleanly, not cluttered
- drills clearly integrated into the side frame structure
- no random pasted machinery next to the board

## Lower background target
The area around and below the board should support the mining theme without becoming visually overwhelming.
Use:
- mine rock walls
- support beams
- rails or timber fragments if they help composition
- crystal clusters
- crates / carts / tools in restrained amounts
- subtle lantern glow

## Bottom HUD target
The bottom HUD should remain functionally the same but visually belong to Ironvein.
It should feel seated inside a mining structure, not floating in blank space.

## Shading / rendering rules
- crisp pixel art
- readable material separation
- avoid airbrushed or blurry gradients
- limited clean shading steps
- strong silhouette readability
- preserve game UI clarity over spectacle
