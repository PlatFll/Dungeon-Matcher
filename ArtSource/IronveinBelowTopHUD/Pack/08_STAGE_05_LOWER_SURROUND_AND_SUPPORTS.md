# Stage 05 — Lower Surround and Support Environment

## Goal
Generate the environment directly around the board and beneath it so the board belongs to the Ironvein zone.

## Needed support art
- rock wall pieces
- timber support pieces
- crystal clusters
- mine props in restrained amounts
- subtle lanterns or brackets if needed
- structural continuation beneath the board

## Visual rule
This area should support the board without overpowering gameplay.
It should feel like the board apparatus is mounted in the mine.

## Prompt direction for PixelLab
Create supporting pixel-art environment pieces for the lower half of a dwarven mining match-3 game screen. Include mine rock, timber supports, subtle crystal clusters, lantern brackets, and structural pieces that make a large board frame feel embedded into a mine shaft. Keep readability high and avoid clutter.

## Deliverable
Integrate these pieces around the board and show the lower half with environment support in place.

## Stop
Stop for review after Stage 05.
