# Scope and Locks

## LOCKED / do not rebuild
The following should stay from the current approved Ironvein top setup:
- top title plate and zone name treatment
- guide button and settings button
- top mine background / combat background
- enemies / enemy positions / combat top layout
- upper lantern logic / upper scene identity
- existing gameplay proportions unless a lower-half connection requires a very small alignment fix

## TARGET AREA TO REBUILD
Rebuild the full lower-half style treatment beginning at the horizontal break **beneath the upper combat scene**.
This includes:
1. the separator ledge / beam just above the board
2. the full board frame
3. drill modules integrated into the board sides
4. support beams / metal brackets / amber crystal insets / bolts
5. the environment immediately around the board
6. lower background area behind the bottom HUD
7. the bottom HUD support styling so it belongs to Ironvein

## Absolute style intent
The lower half should look like it belongs to the same zone as the top:
- mine shafts
- timber beams
- forged metal braces
- rivets / bolts
- amber-orange mine crystals
- warm lantern firelight
- industrial dwarven excavation energy

## What not to do
- Do not regenerate the whole scene in one shot.
- Do not produce painterly or blurry assets.
- Do not place random drills floating beside the board.
- Do not make the drill pieces feel pasted on.
- Do not make the board sides feel like separate stickers.
- Do not overdetail every surface.
- Do not stop before the assigned stage checkpoint.
