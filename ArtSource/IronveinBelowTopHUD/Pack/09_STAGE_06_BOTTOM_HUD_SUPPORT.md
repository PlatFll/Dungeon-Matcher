# Stage 06 — Bottom HUD Support and Seating

## Goal
Make the bottom HUD feel seated inside the Ironvein zone rather than floating.

## Preserve functionality
Do not redesign the functional UI behavior.
Only create the environmental support / seating art around it.

## Desired feel
- forged metal and timber support
- subtle crystal accents
- the main resonance panel should remain readable
- potion / bomb sockets should feel housed in the mine zone
- surrounding support art can include restrained rocks, beams, or crystal fragments

## Prompt direction for PixelLab
Create crisp pixel-art lower HUD support pieces for a dwarven mine themed match-3 interface. Use metal plates, rivets, timber braces, and small amber crystal accents. The pieces should frame the existing bottom HUD cleanly without cluttering it, and should help the HUD feel embedded into the mining environment.

## Deliverable
Assemble the lower HUD support art and show the complete lower-half composition.
