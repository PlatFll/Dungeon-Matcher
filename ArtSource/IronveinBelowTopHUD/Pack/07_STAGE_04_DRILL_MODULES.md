# Stage 04 — Drill Modules Integrated Into Board Sides

## Goal
Generate drill modules that are physically integrated into the board frame sides.

## Very important
The drills must look like **part of the board structure**, not random props beside it.

## Drill design intent
Use inward-facing mining drills with:
- dark steel housings
- bolts and plates
- amber crystal power cores or inset lights
- compact fantasy-industrial dwarven construction
- clear silhouette pointing toward the board

## Layout rule
- one drill per lane position vertically spaced down each side
- separated enough to breathe visually
- mounted into the board-side support assembly
- mirrored cleanly on left and right

## Prompt direction for PixelLab
Create pixel-art side-mounted drill machine modules for a dwarven mining UI frame. Each module is integrated into a vertical support structure and points inward toward a game board. Use dark steel, rivets, mechanical housing, amber crystal power accents, and a compact drill nose. The art should feel handcrafted, sturdy, and clearly part of the frame, not like a separate prop pasted next to it.

## Required generated pieces
- drill module base / mount
- drill nose housing
- crystal-lit connector block
- optional connector brackets / backplates

## Deliverable
Assemble the left and right board side systems with integrated drills in-scene.

## Stop
Stop for review after Stage 04.
