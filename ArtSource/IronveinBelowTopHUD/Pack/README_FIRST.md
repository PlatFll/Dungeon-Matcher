# Ironvein Excavation — Below-Top-HUD PixelLab Build Pack

This pack is for Astra.

## What this pack is for
Rebuild **everything below the TOP HUD** for the Ironvein Excavation zone using **PixelLab text-description generation**, stage by stage, with focused prompts.

## Critical clarification
- **Do NOT regenerate the top HUD.**
- Keep the currently approved Ironvein top section intact:
  - title bar / zone name plate
  - guide button
  - settings button
  - upper mine background / combat scene
  - enemy lineup area
  - portrait frame area if it belongs to the current approved top composition
- The target from the attached mockup is **everything below the top HUD boundary**, especially:
  - the separator strip just above the board
  - the board frame
  - the drill-equipped board sides
  - the board-side structural supports
  - the area behind / around the board
  - the lower background
  - the bottom HUD styling and environment support around it

## Important production rule
Use the attached concept image only as a **composition / target reference for what the lower half should become**.
Do **not** rely on “attach image and hope PixelLab copies it.”
Instead:
1. Describe the needed assets in text.
2. Generate them one category at a time.
3. Clean them to match the game’s coloring/shading rules.
4. Assemble them in Unity.
5. Stop at each review checkpoint.
