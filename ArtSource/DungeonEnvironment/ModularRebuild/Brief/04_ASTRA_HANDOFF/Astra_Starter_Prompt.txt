Use the attached Dungeon Matcher dungeon-scene extraction pack to rebuild the current dungeon battle scene as modular assets.

Read first:
- 00_README.txt
- 03_LAYOUT_AND_MANIFEST/asset_index_and_notes.txt
- 03_LAYOUT_AND_MANIFEST/scene_manifest.json
- 03_LAYOUT_AND_MANIFEST/annotated_scene_map.png
- 01_REFERENCE/full_scene_reference.png

Goal
- Rebuild the scene from separate assets and tiles, not as one flat merged image.
- Preserve the approved shapes, composition, and overall look of the current scene.
- Do not include the characters or the gem sprites in the background asset rebuild.

Requirements
- Treat the screenshot as a displayed-scale reference, not as final native production art.
- Rebuild clean native assets in LibreSprite.
- Preserve the overall design language exactly: chunky purple dungeon masonry, chains, torches, skull banners, stone-and-metal UI frame language, and the current layout.
- Keep the scene recognizable. Do not redesign shapes.
- Simplify only where needed for cleaner native pixel handling.

Deliverables
- Modular asset folder for the dungeon scene
- Rebuilt scene assembled from those parts
- Short validation note listing each asset created and any places where the screenshot crop had to be interpreted manually

Important
- Use the extracted crops as references and slicing guides.
- If a crop is not clean enough to use directly, rebuild it faithfully from the reference.
- Keep everything crisp and grid-aligned.
