What is reusable?

Wall and interior fills repeat. Corners, rails, arch pieces and shallow ledge segments join at their recorded native anchors. Props, ornaments and icons are standalone. Long reference rails and ledge courses are matching parts, not arbitrary Wang tiles. This keeps the supplied contour and layout intact.
