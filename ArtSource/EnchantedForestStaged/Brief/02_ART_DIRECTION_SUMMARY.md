# Art Direction Summary

## Style target
The zone should match the game's polished pixel-art look while preserving the personality of the reference.

## Visual goals
- Magical living forest
- Cozy but adventurous
- Clean, readable, mobile-game-friendly
- Slightly simplified compared with the lush mockup, but still rich and alive

## Must-have visual motifs
- Dense forest depth in the background
- Soft waterfall / bridge / distant grove read if possible, simplified cleanly
- Hanging lanterns integrated into branches
- Leafy wooden frame for the player area
- Organic board surroundings with vine/leaf wrapping
- Mushrooms and stones near the lower corners and side edges
- Wisps / spirit lights that can blink or drift later
- Rune glow accents that are subtle and readable

## What to avoid
- Random hard-surface clutter that feels unplanned
- Over-detailed textures that blur at game scale
- Noisy frame ornamentation
- Too many unique one-off props on the board frame
- Unclear silhouettes or fuzzy generated edges

## Motion planning
Some elements should be designed with future idle motion in mind:
- leaf clusters and hanging vines: light sway
- lanterns: very slight sway or glow flicker
- wisps: blink in/out or tiny hover drift
- tiny motes/fireflies: optional
