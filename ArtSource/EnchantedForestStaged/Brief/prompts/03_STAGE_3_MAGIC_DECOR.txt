STAGE 3 — MAGICAL DECORATION SET

Goal:
Create the magical accent set that makes the forest zone feel alive.

Create these as separate assets:
- hanging lantern
- glowing rune accent / carved rune plaque
- blue wisp / spirit light
- tiny floating sparkle or firefly-like mote
- small vine / hanging leaf strand
- decorative signpost/runepost if useful

Instructions:
1. Use PixelLab one asset family at a time.
2. Match the reference image tone: magical but readable.
3. Wisps should feel soft, ghostly, and friendly.
4. Lanterns should feel integrated into branches or ropes/chains and fit the zone.
5. Clean each asset in LibreSprite.
6. Separate any future-animation components if useful:
   - wisp body/glow
   - lantern idle sway possibilities
   - vine sway variations

Important:
Avoid over-detail and clutter. These are supportive atmospheric pieces.
