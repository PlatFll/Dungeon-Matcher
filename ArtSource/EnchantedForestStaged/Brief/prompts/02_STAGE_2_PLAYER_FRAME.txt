STAGE 2 — PLAYER FRAME / PORTRAIT PANEL

Goal:
Create the forest-themed player portrait frame that matches the reference image and our game's UI logic.

Use:
- `reference/enchanted_forest_match_3_battle_reference.png`

Instructions:
1. Generate the player frame area separately.
2. It should feel like a vertical wooden shrine frame built from bark/wood planks, wrapped with leaves and subtle flowers/vines.
3. Preserve clear readable frame boundaries for gameplay UI.
4. The inner background of the portrait panel should feel distinct from the outer scene. It should not visually merge with the world background.
5. Keep the gem slot area readable and consistent.
6. Clean in LibreSprite:
   - simplify noisy bark texture
   - sharpen edge readability
   - keep corners strong and modular where practical
7. Prepare if possible as modular parts: corners, side segments, top, bottom, inner fill.

Important:
This panel should feel handcrafted and enchanted, not rustic-goofy.
