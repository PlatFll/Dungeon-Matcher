STAGE 6 — BOTTOM HUD AREA ENRICHMENT

Goal:
Make the empty space around the shared bottom HUD feel like part of the enchanted forest, without changing the unified HUD system itself.

Instructions:
1. Preserve the current shared bottom HUD/buttons/panel style.
2. Add only zone-context dressing around it:
   - low leaf clusters
   - soft vine intrusions
   - roots/wood trim if useful
   - small mushrooms or stones near the lower sides
   - subtle magical motes/wisps if they do not hurt readability
3. Keep the HUD fully readable and centered.
4. Avoid overstuffing the bottom area.
5. Clean all added art in LibreSprite.

Important:
This stage is about environmental integration, not redesigning the HUD widgets.
