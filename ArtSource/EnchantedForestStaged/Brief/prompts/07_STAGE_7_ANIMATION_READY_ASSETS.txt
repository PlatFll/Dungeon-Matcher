STAGE 7 — ANIMATION-READY NATURE ASSETS

Goal:
Prepare a small set of forest assets for subtle ambient animation.

Prepare separate assets / variants for:
- swaying leaf clusters
- hanging vine sway pieces
- lantern slight sway/glow flicker support if practical
- blinking / fading wisps
- tiny drifting motes

Instructions:
1. Do not build final long animations here unless already easy.
2. Instead, make sure the assets are separated cleanly for animation.
3. If you do make preview animation frames, keep them very subtle.
4. Clean all assets in LibreSprite.

Important:
The motion must feel light and ambient, like wind and magic, not noisy.
