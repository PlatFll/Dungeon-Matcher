STAGE 1 — TOP BATTLE BACKGROUND

Goal:
Build the enchanted forest top battle background so it feels magical, alive, and coherent with the attached reference image, but slightly simplified to match our game's clean production style.

Use:
- `reference/enchanted_forest_match_3_battle_reference.png`

Instructions:
1. Use PixelLab to generate the top forest battle background only, not the full scene.
2. Focus on the area behind the enemies and around the top HUD.
3. Capture these key traits from the reference:
   - deep layered forest background
   - magical woodland clearing
   - soft distant depth
   - bridge/waterfall/deer-like scenic storytelling if readable
   - hanging lantern ambiance
   - subtle blue magical glow accents
4. Simplify background texture density so it reads clearly at game scale.
5. Keep the combat floor / enemy standing area readable and flatter than the distant background.
6. After generation, clean the result in LibreSprite:
   - remove fuzziness
   - reduce noisy micro-detail
   - keep silhouettes readable
   - align palette to our current game style
7. Present one main candidate and optional alternate if needed.

Important:
Do NOT generate board frame decorations yet. This stage is only the top forest background and enemy-area environment.
