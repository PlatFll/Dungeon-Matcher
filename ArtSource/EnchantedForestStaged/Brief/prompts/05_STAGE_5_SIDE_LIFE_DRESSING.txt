STAGE 5 — SIDE LIFE DRESSING

Goal:
Fill the side spaces around the board with life so the zone does not feel empty.

Create / assemble these supporting assets as appropriate:
- leaf clusters
- branch tips
- vines and hanging creepers
- mushroom clusters
- small stones / mossy rocks
- roots hugging corners or edges
- tiny flowers if subtle and readable

Instructions:
1. Use the reference as direction, but deliberately enrich the side spaces.
2. Focus on the empty side regions around the board and lower sections.
3. These elements should frame the board without interfering with gameplay.
4. Mushrooms and stones should anchor the lower corners.
5. Leaf clusters and branch growth should give the zone a living feel.
6. Clean in LibreSprite and make reusable overlay pieces.
7. Favor layered natural life over random ornaments.

Important:
This stage is specifically meant to make the zone feel less empty and more like a living magical forest.
