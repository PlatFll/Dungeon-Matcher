Remaining visual differences

  • The live game keeps its existing eight-by-eight board, health bars, counters, combat hints and Thaleah labels. Court retains the functional wave counter. Section proportions now give the board more space.
  • The visible reference landmarks and material colors are extracted directly. Areas hidden behind the mockup’s actors, equipment and counters are reconstructed; they cannot be recovered from the flattened PNG. Repairs and tall/wide-screen extensions still differ from the target.
  • The top HUD has an open arena edge and separate thin player frame. Dark stone board cells replace the previous logs/gray cells; character and gem art remain unchanged.
  • The shared bottom HUD has no outer enclosure; individual button rims and accessibility feedback remain. The foreground sides attach independently. Side dressing hides when a viewport has insufficient board gutter. The responsive board uses Point filtering with fractional fitting at some resolutions.
  • Automated checks and desktop Unity captures do not establish physical-device performance or user art approval.
