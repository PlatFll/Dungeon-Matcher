Create a new branch for the magical forest zone rework.

BRANCH NAME:
feature/magical-forest-zone-rework-v2

GOAL:
Rework the magical forest battle scene so it matches Dungeon Matcher’s current polished dungeon-zone structure and readability, while making the forest feel more magical, cleaner, and less goofy than the previous forest mockup.

IMPORTANT HIGH-LEVEL RULES:
1. Keep the overall game layout and gameplay readability consistent with the current dungeon scene.
2. Keep the bottom HUD, settings menu, general buttons, and unified lower UI system the same across zones.
3. Only rework the forest zone’s:
   - top battle background
   - player portrait frame styling for this zone
   - enemy-side battle background
   - board outer frame styling for this zone
   - decorative zone-specific environmental art
4. Do NOT redesign the core gameplay board tiles or gem shapes.
5. Do NOT make the scene over-detailed, blurry, noisy, or cluttered.
6. The forest should feel magical, atmospheric, readable, and premium.
7. The board frame should be surrounded mainly by leaves/vines/wood accents in a deliberate clean way, not random props stuck onto the frame.
8. Add subtle decorative leaf clusters that can later support gentle ambient motion, like leaves moving slightly in wind.
9. The background should feel like a magical forest, not just a normal forest:
   - soft mystical glow
   - ancient woodland mood
   - hints of enchanted runes/lights
   - layered depth
   - subtle fantasy ambience
10. Preserve clean silhouette separation so characters and UI remain easy to read.

VISUAL TARGET:
Use the current dungeon-zone production screen as the structural/quality benchmark.
Use the magical forest mockup as inspiration for theme, mood, and content.
But simplify and polish the forest to match the cleaner, more controlled production style of the dungeon zone.

WHAT NEEDS TO CHANGE FROM THE CURRENT FOREST VERSION:
- Remove the goofy/random-looking board frame decorations.
- Replace them with a cleaner wood-and-leaf frame language.
- Make the magical forest background feel more enchanted and premium.
- Improve cohesion between top HUD area and board area.
- Make the zone feel intentionally art-directed, not procedurally decorated.
- Avoid visual noise.

ART DIRECTION:
FOREST TOP AREA:
- Ancient forest clearing
- Large tree trunks framing the scene
- Soft distant background depth
- Subtle glowing magical accents
- Hanging lanterns are allowed if they feel natural
- Optional faint blue magical wisps/fireflies
- A magical ruin / stone bridge / distant waterfall may appear in the far background, but simplified and not too busy
- Ground where enemies stand should be readable and flatter than the dungeon’s old cracked floor problems
- Keep enemy standing area clean and readable

PLAYER FRAME:
- Forest-themed variant of the player frame
- Material language: aged wood, carved wood, restrained vine growth, leaf accents
- Do not overdecorate
- Keep portrait readability first
- It must still feel like the same game family as the dungeon frame

BOARD FRAME:
- Wooden forest-themed frame with leaves/vines wrapping around it in a controlled way
- Emphasize natural framing using leaf clusters and branch/wood shaping
- Avoid random mushrooms/ornaments all over the frame
- Use a few strong accents, not many weak ones
- Leaves should feel intentionally placed and capable of future ambient animation
- Keep corners, edges, and tiling clean and modular

SIDE DECOR:
- Use restrained magical forest decor:
  - leaves
  - vines
  - lanterns
  - runic carvings
  - small mushrooms near corners only if tasteful
- Avoid clutter

BOTTOM UI:
- KEEP AS IS.
- Do not redesign the lower HUD, resonance panel, potion/bomb button structure, or unified lower menu style.
- Zone should integrate with the existing bottom HUD, not replace it.

5. Does it preserve premium pixel-art clarity?
6. Does it fit with Rattlebones, the gems, and the current dungeon production quality?
