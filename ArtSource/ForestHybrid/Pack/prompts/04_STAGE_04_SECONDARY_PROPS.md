# Stage 4 — Secondary props and polish

## Objective
Create any additional forest props needed to complete the zone cleanly.

## Examples
- lantern variants
- mushrooms
- tiny flowers
- roots / vines
- rune glows
- signpost or carved wood details
- soft magical forest accent sprites

## Rule
Only make what is actually needed.
Do not spam decorative assets.
The goal is controlled richness, not clutter.

## Deliverables
- prop sheet
- where each prop will be used
- updated Unity screenshot

## Stop
Stop for approval after this stage.
