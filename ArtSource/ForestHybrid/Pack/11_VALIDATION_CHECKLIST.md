# Validation checklist

A stage is not approved until these pass:

## Pixel integrity
- crisp native pixels
- no blur
- no resampling damage
- no broken transparency

## Style fit
- feels compatible with Rattlebones and the gem set
- not more detailed than the current dungeon zone by a distracting amount
- not flatter or cheaper-looking than the current dungeon zone

## Assembly quality
- tiles align
- no visible seams unless intentional
- no broken corners
- no cut-off props
- no tangent collisions with gameplay UI

## Readability
- enemy silhouettes remain readable
- stat icons remain readable
- player portrait panel remains readable
- board frame does not crowd gem readability

## Zone consistency
- forest elements feel coherent with each other
- materials are consistent
- palette feels unified
- shared bottom HUD remains unchanged
