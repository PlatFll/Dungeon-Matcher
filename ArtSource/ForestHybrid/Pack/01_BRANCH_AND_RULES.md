# Branch and asset rules

## Branch
Create a new branch:
`feature/forest-zone-hybrid-rebuild`

Do not merge into main.
Do not overwrite the approved dungeon-zone work.
Do not remove shared global UI assets.

## Hard asset rules
- Forest zone must be built as **modular assets**, not a flattened scene image.
- Keep **global bottom HUD, shared buttons, and settings/menu assets unchanged**.
- Forest work is limited to zone-specific art:
  - upper background / enemy battle area background
  - player frame zone theming
  - board frame zone theming
  - side decorations and forest environmental dressing
- Preserve gameplay layout proportions as much as possible.
- Preserve readability of characters, gems, enemy health bars, and stat glyphs.
- Do not let forest decoration make the combat area noisy or muddy.

## Pixel quality rules
- No blurry scaling.
- No softened edges.
- No painterly smearing.
- No over-detailed micro-noise clusters.
- No massive light/shadow clumps that fight character readability.
- Keep pixel edges crisp and game-native.

## Stop rules
Stop after each stage for review.
