# Art direction summary

## Intent
The Magical Forest zone should feel like a **forest reinterpretation of our now-good dungeon zone**, not like an unrelated mockup pasted into the game.

## Primary goals
1. Match the current dungeon zone's readability and clean game-native construction.
2. Translate the magical forest mockup into a simplified, production-ready zone.
3. Keep the zone visually rich but less dense than the forest mockup.
4. Preserve the user's sprite/gem readability first.

## Forest-specific visual language
- warm carved wood
- old forest shrine / druidic structure feel
- restrained hanging vines and roots
- subtle glowing runes / magical marks
- lanterns, mushrooms, flowers, carved posts, leaf trim
- calm layered forest depth in the far background
- clear ground strip for enemies to stand on
- no heavy clutter behind enemy silhouettes

## Simplification target
Compared with the forest mockup, reduce:
- tiny leaf noise
- excessive foliage density
- over-busy side dressing
- hyper-detailed terrain texture

Keep:
- strong forest identity
- magical ambience
- wood-and-vine frame language
- waterfall/deer/forest-depth mood if it can be suggested cleanly in the distant background

## Style fit target
The forest zone should feel like it belongs in the same product as:
- current dungeon scene
- Rattlebones sprite
- current gem set
- shared bottom HUD
