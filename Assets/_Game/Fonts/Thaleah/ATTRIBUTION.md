# Thaleah

Thaleah Fat by Rick Hoppmann (Tiny Worlds).

Source: https://tinyworlds.itch.io/free-pixel-font-thaleah

License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The original TTF is unmodified. Dungeon Matcher generates a monochrome bitmap
TextMesh Pro atlas at 16 points, and maps unsupported typographic punctuation
to the corresponding Thaleah ASCII glyph. Attribution also appears in the game guide.
