CURRENT IMPLEMENTATION USED FOR CALIBRATION
- Player_Skeleton.asset: level-1 gem damage11, HP100. Bardley:10, HP80.
  Gideon:10.5, HP90. Ordinary 3-gem weakness packets therefore resolve to
  35 /30 /30 damage respectively after the current rounding sequence.
- CombatController.CalculateGemClearDamage multiplies gem count by player
  damage and the cascade multiplier, then rounds to integer before upgrades.
  Game.unity has cascadeDamageBonusPerDepth0.2. EnemyActor applies final
  CombatAmounts.Round: nearest5, positive minimum5, zero stays0.
  Example Rattlebones3 at depth1:33*1.2=39.6 ->40 ->40 final.
- Matching 3/4/5/L/T clears yield5/7/10/10/10 energy when they damage a matching
  enemy; off-target1/3/5/5/5. Special clears give2 per matching-damage gem,
  otherwise1. Ability clears need explicit entitlement; Bardley's cast refund
  is capped. Energy is NOT rounded to5.
- Ability costs: Royal Decree100, Cracked Gems80, ChronoShutter100. A plan
  cannot assume the player always has an interrupting ability available.
- Basics currently use real seconds. Specials use completed valid moves.
  Mender channels and forest vines do not exist in EnemySpecialAbilityKind.
  Merely filling an EnemyDefinition cannot implement them.
- Existing stagger thresholds: Normal30%, Special45%, Miniboss65%, Boss90%
  of maximum HP. Grace/drain/stagger/immunity currently use seconds. The old
  StaggerCombatBridge is disabled. Preserve the active owner's damage inputs.
- DifficultyProfile_Standard: HP/damage grow1% per global wave through100;
  attack speed and special cadence curves stay flat, player-power correction
  is disabled. Prototype examples below use factor1, level1, no cards or zone
  resonance. At global wave31 factor1.30 applies before final5-point rounding.
  Do not silently reset difficulty at a forest transition.
- Existing limits:6 global chain/pin restrictions,10 structures,4 mined cells.
  Forest vines/anchors must share restriction accounting, not add six on top.

SCOUT — PRESSURE THROUGH FREQUENCY
No special ability (None), no follow-up, no blocker. One5-damage arrow every
two accepted moves. A matching level1 clear removes roughly half its HP.
Response: use its displayed weakness, kill it before repeated shots, or accept
small frequent hits while solving the more important support target.

TRAILGUARD — PRESSURE THROUGH A BIGGER SINGLE HIT
No special ability (None), no follow-up, no blocker. One15-damage maul hit
every four moves. A full countdown gives room to plan a special or heal.
Response: exploit its weakness or stagger before impact; a shield can gate the
whole hit using existing rules. Do not split this into three hits to defeat
shield gating. Three ordinary matching clears kill it at the fixture baseline.

MENDER — STABLE, INTERRUPTIBLE ALLY HEAL
Proposed kind ChannelHeal, implemented later as IEnemySpecialAbilityRuntime
registered by the existing factory. isSupport=true, isBoardDisruptor=false.
Never reuse RoyalArchbishop wholesale: its rune/triage/benediction kit differs.

Initial eligibility: after two completed accepted moves since spawn. At each
later safe boundary, if ready and not committed, select a living OTHER ally
whose HP/maxHP is STRICTLY less than0.75. Self-targeting is disabled.
Choose lowest HP fraction; ties use persistent encounter actor ID/spawn order.
Record target ID plus spawn generation, never just slot. Keep that target.

Begin after actionN settles. Show a visible link to the chosen ally, pale-green
palm/staff cue and a channel bar labelled "HEAL · 2 MOVES". N does not count.
After N+1 resolves show1; after N+2 resolves, process player damage, deaths,
stagger and prior poison consequences first. Only a surviving uninterrupted
caster heals that same living recipient by20HP, capped to missing health.
Use EnemyActor.RestoreHealth and actual accepted amount for the green +number.
Being hit alone is not an interrupt. An actual stagger event or caster death
cancels once. Recipient death/slot replacement fizzles; never silently retarget.
If another effect fills the target, completion may restore0; no fake +20 or
new target. Leaving the encounter cancels all links and pending completions.

No basics during channel, release presentation or recovery. Pause existing
basic progress; do not reset it to full or allow a queued hit through the cast.
After success, fizzle or interruption, enter two FUTURE accepted moves of
recovery. Recovery expiry happens after that tick's action-selection phase;
earliest new action is the following tick. A longer active stagger also gates
readiness. No same-tick heal and basic attack; no catch-up burst afterward.

Example: trigger afterN=3; response actions4 and5; success/cancel after5;
recovery on6 and7; eligible again at8. A kill/stagger during action5 wins over
the heal. A free skill may interrupt without itself advancing this countdown.

Counterplay: match the Mender's shown weakness, use a ready special/skill,
finish the recipient, or deliberately let a20 heal resolve while killing a
larger threat. With75HP, Special stagger threshold is33.75: one35 packet can
interrupt; two30 packets can interrupt if retained by the proposed move-based
meter policy. This is an illustration, not a claim that seconds-based stagger
has already been converted. Formation validation must find a useful response
without requiring a charged skill or a particular gem color by assumption.

ROOTBINDER — THE SHARED FOREST VINE
Proposed VineCaster kind through the same runtime factory. BoardController
owns placement, warnings, occupancy, gravity, clears and cleanup. No separate
Rootbinder-only blocker grid, direct dictionary writes or independent resolver.
Reuse movable-pin infrastructure with a Vine style/lifetime payload. Existing
top-up helper clamps owner pins1..3 and only tops up; extend a generic queued
placement request for exact warning targets/placement count in Phase4/5.

First readiness after3 completed moves. Warn two distinct safe ordinary gems
for one FUTURE accepted move; bind surviving eligible targets after that move.
Up to2 placements per cast, owner cap3, shared restriction cap6 including vines,
chains, freezes and protective anchor markers; pending reservations count.
Recurrence4 future moves after a successful commit, not after a failed attempt.
If fewer safe cells exist, place only the eligible subset. If none, retain
readiness and retry at a later accepted boundary, not every Update frame.
Warning clears, target replacement, caster kill/stagger or encounter exit
cancel their specific pending placements. No surprise retarget at expiry.

Vine: cannot be manually swapped, can fall with its gem, can participate in
matches initiated around it; normal direct clears, special destruction and
the existing conversion/replacement release path remove it. Adjacent clears
do NOT automatically remove a movable chain/vine. Do not impose a new
one-hit-per-move rule on clears, bombs or special chaining.

An untouched seed placed after actionN gets two full future responses. At N+1
reserve/show a safe orthogonally adjacent child target; at N+2 bind it only if
parent/target/owner remain valid. Clearing parent or child target cancels this
spread. The new child gets age0; no recursive spread in its birth action.
Parent age resets after success/fizzle; cap/unsafe refusal also restarts the
two-move warning window, preventing a surprise delayed avalanche.
At most ONE successful spread across all forest vines per accepted action in
the first test profile, selected by oldest seed then stable ID. This is a
growth budget, not a cap on player damage/clears. All sources share the cap6.

Rootbinder-owned seeds and descendants carry its persistent owner ID and are
released on its death/encounter end through queued owner cleanup. Environment
seeds use a separate visit owner and survive enemy death until their declared
visit cleanup. Pending target reservations release on every cancellation.
Initial test formations use Rootbinder OR environment seeding, then combine
only after shared-budget and useful-response tests pass.

Response: clear the warned gem, clear a bound gem via a match/special, interrupt
the caster's warning, or kill the caster to remove its entire vine family.
Keep gem faces visible; thorn-shaped hostile markers differ from frame leaves.

SHARED ORDER AND SAVE REQUIREMENTS
One accepted manual action ID; finish BoardController's player cascade and
all attributed resource changes first. Then deaths/owner cleanup and actual
interrupts; prior poison ticks; due channels/warnings; ready specials; basics.
Stable persistent actor IDs resolve ties. A committed actor gets one action
slot; summons/new casts/new vines never consume their birth action as response.
Thinking, failed swaps, inspection, pause, free skills/supplies, every cascade
step and enemy-generated cascades contribute zero extra ticks.
Save ruleset/schema, action ID/phase, tick, actor+target IDs, channel state and
due/recovery counters, paused basic progress, vine age/parent/owner/reservations,
RNG and consumed consequence IDs. Restore without offline catch-up or retarget.
Keep legacy seconds saves in a declared compatibility profile until a tested
migration exists. ChronoShutter restores the board, not combat deadlines/procs.
Full migration matrix remains Phase1/05_CLOCK_MIGRATION_CHECKLIST.md. This
phase does not implement the move clock, stagger conversion or new abilities.
