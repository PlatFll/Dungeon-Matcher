BARKHIDE WARDEN / LOCAL MINIBOSS
Old orc sentinel, broad bark-armored shoulders, single tall bark shield, exposed
face/tusks, practical ochre cloth. A protective role reads through the slab and
stance; he carries no sword. 64x64 native canvas, bottom-center pivot, row63.
Palette14 colors after material cleanup. Source geometry retained exactly.

Prototype:150HP,15 basic damage every4 accepted moves, threat5.
Guarding Roots uses two clearly linked root anchors, drawn as the SAME canonical
vine marker with a small anchor knot. They are status-marked ordinary gems,
not a new structural blocker class. Anchors do not spread; ordinary vines can.
They count toward the same6-restriction budget and existing safe-cell checks.
First warning after2 moves; one future move to clear either warned gem or
stagger/kill the caster. Commit only surviving targets. One or both anchors
provide a single25% damage reduction to the Warden, never stacking per anchor.
Never grant invulnerability or secretly protect escorts. HP and shield remain
separate; this is an owned damage modifier through the central damage flow.
When the last anchor is cleared, remove protection and open a two-future-action
exposure: +25% incoming weakness damage, final rounding once. Exposure begins
after the clearing action finishes, so its two full damage windows are N+1/N+2.
Free skills may benefit during the open window but do not advance its deadline.
After exposure, wait three future moves before another one-move anchor warning.
Stagger cancels preparation; on interruption enter the same exposure/recovery.
Caster death removes all owned anchors/reservations and modifiers immediately
through authoritative cleanup. No heal or second phase added.
Counterplay: clear a linked anchor, interrupt preparation, use the exposed
window, or use enough raw damage to kill through reduction. Strong builds may
skip the mechanic; no minimum ritual count, phase gate or HP padding.
Suggested first fixture: Warden + Scout. Later: Warden + Trailguard. Do not
add Mender in the first lesson or overlap a second major warning deadline.

BRIAR MATRIARCH / REGIONAL APEX CANDIDATE
Elder elf with a narrow long silhouette, silver braid, three branch crown prongs,
wine cloth, pale open sleeves and seed-cage staff. This extends the accepted elf
language without copying a royal bishop. Declared96x96 native canvas, same
source pixel density; measured occupied width53, height79. Tool drawing was
translated down11 pixels, without scaling or lost pixels, to source row95.
Use bottom-center pivot(0.5,0); the larger canvas adds vertical room. The native
comparison and three-slot proof keep a common source-pixel scale and floor.
This canvas is a proposed apex exception, not a new ordinary-character standard.

Prototype:240HP,10 basic damage every4 moves, threat7, isSupport=true and
isBoardDisruptor=true. Boss category alone must never cause teleport; the
encounter's designated apex-completion flag owns that decision.
Her ritual combines two taught ideas, not a menu of new phase rules:
1) After three moves, begin a TWO-future-move Renew the Grove channel.
2) Show two linked seed-anchor target gems plus one fixed wounded ally target.
   Prefer another living ally below75%; self-target only if none qualifies.
   This declared self fallback distinguishes the apex, not the starter Mender.
3) Each uncleared rune at completion contributes10HP to that chosen target,
   at most20 total. Clearing both cancels all healing. Kill/stagger also cancels.
   Recipient death fizzles; no retarget. No autoattack during the ritual.
4) Empty/cleared ritual anchors or stagger interrupt open the SAME +25%
   two-future-action exposure as Warden; completed ritual has two moves of
   ordinary recovery. Recurrence only after recovery then three future moves.
Rune/anchor visuals, ownership, warning response and removal reuse the canonical
vine payload, with nonspreading ritual lifetime. Do not create an apex-only
obstacle engine or add a damage reflection mechanic. Persistent root protection
is taught by Warden; Matriarch recombines its linked targets with Mender's channel.
No invulnerability thresholds, forced reinforcement at fixed HP or mandatory
minimum number of casts. The whole formation can be defeated at any time.
First apex fixture: Matriarch + Scout + Trailguard, total threat10.5. Later test
Rootbinder replacing ONE escort under the same shared pressure budget. Mender
plus apex double-heal is excluded from the initial test matrix.
Clearing the designated apex formation is the future reward/transition trigger;
wait for all surviving escorts, not the boss's rank or death alone. Destination
selection remains random among ready regions, not a player route choice.
