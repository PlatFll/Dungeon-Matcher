# Future zone concept locks

Approved 2026-10-06. **Documentation only.** No assets, enemies, effects, encounters
or zone runtime are authorized here. Numerical values are provisional.

## Reef Clan major boss placeholder

Queen Nacre remains the Nacre Court boss. A separate future Reef Clan major boss
may enter by finishing off a randomly chosen valid small sea-folk enemy already
occupying a slot, expressing survival-of-the-fittest authority. If no valid victim
exists, use an alternate entrance. Do not create a victim just for the scene.

That death belongs to the boss intro. It gives no player kill/damage credit,
rewards, energy or player-kill procs, and must preserve wave completion accounting.
Name, design, valid-victim taxonomy and complete kit remain unresolved.

## Dwarven Cave

Brittle Stone becomes Hardened Stone when neglected, then Obsidian after further
neglect. Obsidian is provisionally around three hits. No timings are finalized.

Persistent drills sit at appropriate board edges with visible charge meters.
Player matches adjacent to, or feeding, the relevant lane charge them. Dwarves
mine magical ore for a shared industrial network powering turrets, processors,
furnaces and other equipment. Powering their own machine can also contribute to
shared drill capacity; an enemy may accidentally finish charging a useful drill.

A charged drill fires through its full legal row/column, destroying gems/objects
and all three stone stages in its legal path. Drills differ from ordinary bombs
because they are persistent infrastructure influenced by both sides. Dwarven
machinery changes board contents; future Steampunk machinery should primarily
change board arrangement. Detailed lore and implementations remain gaps.

## Snowy Mountain

Frost progresses to Heavy Ice and then Bifrost when neglected. Clear manageable
ice early or accept a more dangerous late state. The evolving ice is the pressure;
do not add a separate temperature meter.

Deliberately breaking Bifrost through a player-made clear retaliates with player
damage and applies Frostbite, temporarily increasing incoming damage. Cascades
and appropriate specials should clear it safely or substantially more safely.
Exact durability, progression cadence, retaliation values and special exceptions
remain provisional. The player status is Frostbite, not Freeze: it does not imply
loss of control. No Snowy Mountain runtime is part of this revision.
