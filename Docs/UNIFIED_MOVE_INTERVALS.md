# Unified combat: provisional roster intervals

Current new-run profile: `unified-accepted-moves-v3`. Each definition contains
explicit opening/repeat/readiness values. Old v2 and second-based saves keep
their original fields. Haste rounds to the nearest whole interval in v3, retaining its two-move floor; v2 keeps ceiling rounding. Authored multi-hit
sequences, warning response windows and board-placement caps are unchanged.
Most abilities now charge in 2–4 moves; Berserker rage remains an HP trigger.
Numbers for enemies without a special are unused. Rootbinder and forest leaders
use their v3 definition after a cast; King's enraged charge drops from 3 to 2.
