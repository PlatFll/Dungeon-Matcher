## Original Dungeon enemies

**Farmer and Pan Villager.** Farmer’s actual user-edited nine-cel idle became the selected source. The connected body, hat, blink, hands and pitchfork are the precedent for following the body through a loop. Pan Villager uses a grounded compression, the pan and gripping arm moving together, plus restrained scarf-tip follow-through. In the native attacks Farmer thrusts the pitchfork and Pan Villager swings the repaired cool-iron pan overhead. The eight-exposure attacks in `ArtSource/CombatActions/README.md` share a 320 ms contact and 680 ms total. The early 96×64 enemy cells used symmetric side room for the tool. The Farmer idle adoption in that record follows the earlier `ArtSource/CombatIdles/README.md` polish.

**Miner, Basket Villager and Barricade Villager.** `ArtSource/LocalEnemies/README.md` identifies the Miner’s tool side-swing and jumping ground strike, the Basket Villager’s berry release, and the Barricade Villager’s axe strike and quick build. The Miner’s supplied native source and PNG were different versions. The native ASE was the chosen acting source. For the idle family, the saved selected version supersedes the stronger earlier pass. A deliberate lift in the Miner’s ground-strike ability has a different purpose from his planted idle.

**Crossbow Guard, Barricade Guard, Spear Guard and Siege Sergeant.** `ArtSource/GuardIdles/README.md` records the approved restoration and the subsequent Crossbow Guard bow-contour/shoulder-join repair. The other three retained their selected files. These four set the accepted restrained idle rhythm for the larger family. The approved basic and special motion records give Crossbow Guard an aim/recoil and Chain Shot, Barricade Guard a compact hammer swing and wall raise, Spear Guard one straight thrust, and Siege Sergeant a two-stage heave and heavy hammer contact plus his authored ability.

**Other named Dungeon actors.** `ArtSource/RemainingCast/SelectedIdles/README.md` records the accepted nine 130 ms exposures above anchored feet, coherent head travel, sleeve overlap and restrained cloth for Town Marshal, the four knights, six royal units and King. The King settles less. `ArtSource/EnemyAttacks/prompts.json` records the individual motion requests. The selection and native edit records below establish which drawings reached production.

Follow the later tail revision for Hammerhead Bruiser and Breakwater Captain, and respect the restored Puffer Sentinel face. The body and tail root must move together.
